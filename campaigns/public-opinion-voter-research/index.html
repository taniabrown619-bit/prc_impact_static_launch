The Qualitative Difference

Understand the “why” behind the response.

Organizations comparing public opinion research firms often need two different kinds of evidence. Quantitative polling measures responses across a defined sample. Qualitative research investigates how people reason, what experiences shape their views, and why language lands differently across audiences.

PRC Impact's work on this page is the qualitative side of that equation. The goal is not to turn a conversation into a percentage. It is to give leaders a disciplined explanation of what people mean, what remains unresolved, and what the team should consider next. For a broader guide to framing the decision and choosing a method, explore PRC's qualitative research studies.

Common Questions

Before you commission the research.

Is PRC Impact a polling firm?

The service described here is qualitative public-opinion and voter research, not quantitative polling. PRC Impact uses structured conversations and analysis to explain the reasoning, language, and context behind audience views.

Who commissions this kind of research?

PRC Impact works with campaigns, civic organizations, coalitions, public agencies, nonprofits, and public-affairs teams that need to understand an audience before making a public-impact decision.

Which methods can be used?

The verified qualitative methods include focus groups, in-depth interviews, listening sessions, message and issue research, and structured analysis. The final method should follow the decision, audience, timing, and evidence needed.

Can the research support message development?

Yes. Qualitative message and issue research can explore how audiences understand language, framing, ideas, and positioning before communications or initiatives move forward. It does not promise a campaign or policy outcome.

Does PRC Impact work nationally?

Yes. PRC Impact is based in Montgomery, Alabama and serves organizations across the South and nationally. Virtual and in-person research are available when the engagement and logistics support them.

What happens after the conversations are complete?

PRC Impact identifies patterns, differences, tensions, and implications, then translates the findings into clear recommendations for the decision at hand. To discuss the next step, use the project inquiry form below.
