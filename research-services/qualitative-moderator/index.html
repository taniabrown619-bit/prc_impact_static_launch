Direct answer

What does a qualitative moderator do?

A qualitative moderator guides a structured conversation so participants can explain their own experiences and reasoning. The moderator follows the study's questions, asks neutral follow-ups, manages group dynamics when needed and leaves room for unexpected insight.

PRC can join a study designed by a research firm or organization, or help shape the wider research plan. Those are different engagements. We will define who owns recruitment, the discussion guide, consent, recording, analysis and the final report before a project starts.

Defined moderation support

Your team provides the research framework. PRC moderates agreed sessions using a reviewed discussion guide and a clear handoff for recordings or notes. Any guide development, recruitment or synthesis should be named in the scope if needed.

  • Focus groups or one-to-one interviews
  • Virtual or in-person delivery as agreed
  • Session plan, participant care and neutral probing

Full research partnership

If the decision, audience or method is still open, PRC can discuss question framing, study design, fieldwork, analysis and recommendations as a broader engagement. See the qualitative research studies guide for that path.

  • Research questions and method selection
  • Fieldwork and interpretation
  • Decision-ready findings within an agreed scope
Before the first session

A useful moderator brief is specific.

01

Name the decision

Tell us what the research will inform and what the study can realistically answer.

02

Define participants

Explain the audience, recruitment criteria, accessibility needs and who will handle invitations.

03

Review the guide

Agree on opening language, key topics, probes, timing and any consent or recording requirements.

04

Specify the handoff

Decide how session notes, observations and synthesis responsibilities will be transferred.

The CDC Field Epidemiology Manual describes semi-structured groups and individual interviews as flexible ways to explore context and meaning. That general method guidance does not validate any particular project result.

Common scope questions

Questions to settle before booking.

Can a moderator work with a discussion guide our team created?

Yes, when the guide and roles are reviewed together. A pre-fieldwork review can identify unclear wording, timing problems or missing probes without assuming PRC owns the full study.

Does a moderator-only assignment include recruiting participants?

Only if recruitment is explicitly included in the scope. Name who recruits, screens, confirms, compensates and communicates with participants before sessions begin.

When should we commission a full qualitative study instead?

Consider a broader study when the research question, audience, method, analysis plan or final decision use still needs to be defined.
