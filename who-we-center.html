Who We Center — Audiences & Contexts

The people closest to a decision often understand its real impact first.

This is not advocacy. It is a research lens.

We work with institutions, agencies, nonprofits, coalitions, and campaigns that need to understand the people their decisions affect before they commit to a direction.

Start a Project

Core Audiences

  • Black residents and families

    Education, health, civic life, public safety, and community investment.

  • Women, mothers, and caregivers

    Care, family stability, health decisions, and systems navigation.

  • Voters and civic audiences

    Public investment, issue campaigns, civic trust, and message clarity.

  • Rural and Southern communities

    Place, access, infrastructure, and institutional trust.

  • People directly affected by public systems

    Residents, patients, families, students, and service users navigating public systems.

Where This Work Applies

Public Investment

Bond measures, tax referendums, and capital plans where voter trust and community understanding shape whether public dollars move.

Civic Strategy & Engagement

Coalition building, voter engagement, and civic campaigns where outreach has to land with audiences that polling and panels routinely misread.

Health, Care, & Public Systems

Programs and services that depend on caregivers, patients, and families understanding what they are being asked to do — and trusting the institution behind it.

Disability, Autism, & Education Access

Research into how families navigate public systems for special education, autism support, and disability services: what they know, what they are told, and where the system fails them.

This is a research context, not a service line.

Public Safety & Community Strategy

Violence prevention, neighborhood investment, and community planning where the people most affected hold information the planning process needs.
