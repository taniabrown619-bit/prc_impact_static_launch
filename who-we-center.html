Who We Center

Research shaped by audience and context.

PRC Impact conducts research across sectors and populations, bringing focused experience to communities and public audiences whose perspectives are essential to sound decisions.

We design the research around how people experience an issue, the language they use, the institutions they encounter, and the context shaping their response.

Why Context Matters in Research

Who participates shapes what the research can reveal.

People respond to policies, programs, messages, and institutions through the context of their own experiences. Strong qualitative research accounts for that from the beginning.

RecruitmentReaching people whose experience is relevant to the question.

Research designAsking questions in language and sequences that make sense to participants.

FacilitationCreating the conditions for candor, trust, and more complete responses.

InterpretationUnderstanding what the findings mean within the environment that shaped them.

Across our work, we prioritize the perspectives of people directly affected by the decision, program, issue, or public system being studied.
