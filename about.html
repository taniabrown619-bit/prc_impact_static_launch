About — The Firm & The Founder

The best public-impact decisions begin with honest listening.

Tania Johns founded PRC Impact on that conviction. The firm is small by design. Engagements are led personally, not handed off, from research design through final recommendations.

For larger engagements, PRC Impact can bring in trusted research, recruitment, facilitation, and analysis support while keeping strategy, design, and final interpretation senior-led.

Founder & Principal

Tania Johns

Founder and Principal · Qualitative Researcher · Civic Strategist · RIVA-Trained Moderator

Tania Johns is the founder and principal of PRC Impact, where she leads qualitative research and civic strategy for public-impact decisions.

Her work spans public investment, health communications, violence prevention planning, voter engagement, and community planning. Across these areas, she helps clients understand how people think, decide, respond, and experience the issues they face.

Her civic background includes serving as Deputy Campaign Manager for the historic campaign that elected Montgomery’s first Black mayor, prior experience at a national polling and public opinion research firm, and leadership roles in multi-state civic engagement work.

Tania is a graduate of Alabama State University and holds a Master of Public Administration from Auburn University at Montgomery, with a concentration in healthcare administration and policy.

She is a RIVA-trained focus group moderator and qualitative researcher.

Her AI training includes certification in AI consulting, prompt design, and instructional design, with ongoing study in AI compliance infrastructure, responsible AI governance, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and the EU AI Act.

She is based in Montgomery, Alabama, and works with clients across the South and nationally.

Better decisions begin with better listening. If you have a project where that matters, we should talk.
