About PRC Impact

A qualitative research and civic strategy firm grounded in public life.

PRC Impact designs and leads qualitative research for public agencies, nonprofits, coalitions, campaigns, and organizations making decisions that affect people and communities.

We conduct focus groups, interviews, listening sessions, and message and issue research, then translate the findings into practical direction for policy, public affairs, communications, engagement, and strategy.

Our work brings together research discipline, public-opinion experience, civic understanding, and close attention to the context shaping how people respond.

Tania Johns

Founder and Principal

Connect on LinkedInSpeaking and Briefings

Tania Johns leads PRC Impact's qualitative research, bringing experience across public opinion, civic strategy, community engagement, and decisions involving public institutions.

Her background includes public-opinion research at Anzalone Liszt Grove Research, now Impact Research, leadership in multi-state civic engagement, and senior roles in Montgomery politics. She was Deputy Campaign Manager for the campaign that elected Montgomery's first Black mayor and later advised the mayor's administration.

Her work has supported public investment, health communications, violence prevention planning, voter engagement, and community planning. Across these areas, she helps clients understand how people interpret issues, make choices, and respond to institutions and messages.

Tania is a graduate of Alabama State University and holds a Master of Public Administration from Auburn University at Montgomery, with a concentration in healthcare administration and policy. She is a RIVA-trained qualitative moderator and Certified AI Consultant.
