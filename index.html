Research that shapes decisions people can stand behind.

We design and lead qualitative research that helps leaders understand how the people closest to a decision think, decide, and respond.

The work is senior-led and built for organizations working through consequential decisions.

The Audiences We Center

We bring particular depth to research with communities whose perspectives are often essential to public-impact decisions.

  • Black residents and families

    Education, health, civic life, public safety, and community investment.

  • Women, mothers, and caregivers

    Care, family stability, health decisions, and systems navigation.

  • Voters and civic audiences

    Public investment, issue campaigns, civic trust, and message clarity.

  • Rural and Southern communities

    Place, access, infrastructure, and institutional trust.

  • People directly affected by public systems

    Residents, patients, families, students, and service users navigating public systems.

What We Do

Four service lines.

Qualitative Research Studies

End-to-end research designed around the decision you need to make.

Focus Groups, Interviews, and Listening Sessions

Senior-led facilitation with the people whose perspectives need to be understood.

Message and Issue Research

Testing how language, framing, and positioning land before they go public.

Strategy From the Findings

Clear recommendations that help turn research into decisions, messages, engagement plans, and next steps.
