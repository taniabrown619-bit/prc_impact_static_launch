Research Services

Research designed around the decision it needs to inform.

PRC Impact can lead a complete qualitative study or provide focused support within a larger research engagement.

Our work supports public policy, public affairs, civic participation, nonpartisan political and issue research, education, health, and community investment.

Research Design and Study Development

We define the research questions, identify the audiences that need to be heard, select the right methods, and develop the study materials and fieldwork plan.

Question framing, sampling, screeners, discussion guides, and study planning

Focus Groups, Interviews, and Listening Sessions

We lead structured conversations that move beyond initial responses and uncover the experiences, language, concerns, and considerations shaping how people think.

RIVA-trained moderation for virtual and in-person research

Message and Issue Research

We examine how audiences understand an issue and respond to language, framing, ideas, and positioning before communications or initiatives move forward.

Message testing, issue exploration, and audience response

Analysis, Interpretation, and Recommendations

We identify the patterns, differences, tensions, and implications across the research, then explain what the findings mean for the decision at hand.

Findings, interpretation, recommendations, and presentations
From Findings to Direction

Research should make the next decision clearer.

PRC Impact identifies what people understand, what shapes their response, where trust or communication breaks down, and what organizations may be missing.

Clients receive findings and recommendations they can use to strengthen:

  • Public policy and program decisions
  • Nonpartisan civic, political, and issue work
  • Public affairs and public-facing communications
  • Community and stakeholder engagement
  • Organizational planning and strategy

PRC Impact provides the research and interpretation. Clients use that direction to make decisions, develop policy, communicate, and engage their audiences.
