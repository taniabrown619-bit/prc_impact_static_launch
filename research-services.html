How We Work

  1. Clarify the decision

    We begin with the decision, message, or strategy the research needs to inform.

  2. Design the study

    We shape the audience, method, questions, and fieldwork around the context.

  3. Listen carefully

Frequently Asked Questions

A few of the questions clients ask before we begin.

What types of organizations does PRC Impact work with?

PRC Impact works with public agencies, nonprofits, coalitions, campaigns, foundations, and mission-driven organizations making decisions that affect communities, public trust, or civic life. We are a good fit for organizations that need more than surface feedback and want research that can inform strategy, messaging, planning, or engagement.

What kinds of research do you conduct?

We conduct qualitative research, including focus groups, interviews, listening sessions, stakeholder conversations, message testing, issue research, and field-based studies. Each engagement is designed around the decision, audience, and context in front of us.

What is qualitative research?

Qualitative research helps organizations understand how people think, feel, decide, interpret language, and experience an issue. It is especially useful when a decision depends on trust, context, communication, public support, or lived experience.

What is the difference between a focus group and a listening session?

A focus group is usually a structured research conversation with a specific audience and discussion guide. A listening session may be more open-ended and is often used when organizations need to understand community experience, concerns, or context before making decisions. Both can be useful. The right method depends on the question you need answered.

Do you only conduct focus groups?

No. Focus groups are one method. We also conduct interviews, listening sessions, stakeholder conversations, message and issue research, and strategy sessions based on findings. The method depends on what you need to learn and who needs to be heard.

Do you provide moderator-only support?

Yes. PRC Impact can provide moderator-only support for organizations, agencies, or research partners that already have a study design, screener, recruitment process, or discussion guide in place. We also provide full-service qualitative research from design through final recommendations.

Where AI Fits in Our Work

We use AI where it sharpens analysis. We do not use it to replace the listening itself.

Qualitative research depends on what is said, what is unsaid, and what becomes visible only when a senior researcher pays close attention.

We use AI to support analysis, identify patterns across transcripts, surface themes, and stress-test interpretation. It accelerates rigor. It does not replace it.

Clients know exactly how we are working with their data. Tools in use, what stays in our hands, and how sensitive material is handled are documented before the engagement begins.
